  <h1>NutriSnap Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p class="date">Last updated: April 26, 2026</p>

  <h2>Overview</h2>
  <p>NutriSnap is a personal nutrition tracking app. All your personal and health data is
  stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers and do not upload your personal data
   to the cloud.</p>

  <h2>Data We Collect</h2>
  <p>Information you enter directly:</p>
  <ul>
  <li>Body metrics: weight, height, age, activity level, goal weight</li>
  <li>Food and nutrition logs, calorie and macro data</li>
  <li>Water intake and fasting session times</li>
  <li>Dietary preferences and wellness goals</li>
  </ul>
  <p>With your explicit permission, via Apple HealthKit:</p>
  <ul>
  <li>Active energy burned, step count, heart rate, body weight, blood glucose</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>How We Use Your Data</h2>
  <ul>
  <li>To calculate daily calorie targets, macro goals, and BMI</li>
  <li>To display nutrition summaries and progress charts</li>
  <li>To generate meal recommendations based on your diet preference</li>
  <li>To track fasting sessions and wellness challenges</li>
  <li>To sync between iPhone and Apple Watch</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>What We Do Not Do</h2>
  <ul>
  <li>We do not sell your data to third parties</li>
  <li>We do not share your health data with advertisers</li>
  <li>We do not require account creation</li>
  <li>We do not use your data for advertising</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Third-Party Services</h2>
  <p><strong>Google Gemini</strong> — When you use the AI Snap feature, your food photo is sent
   to Google's API for analysis. Only the photo is transmitted — no personal information.
  Subject to <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google's Privacy Policy</a>.</p>
  <p><strong>USDA FoodData Central</strong> — Food text search queries only. No personal data
  transmitted.</p>
  <p><strong>Open Food Facts</strong> — Barcode numbers only. No personal data transmitted.</p>

  <h2>Data Storage</h2>
  <p>All personal and health data is stored locally on your device using Apple's CoreData
  framework, encrypted by your device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. We cannot access data
  stored on your device.</p>

  <h2>HealthKit</h2>
  <p>HealthKit data is never used for advertising and is never shared with third parties, in
  compliance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines. You can revoke access at any time in iPhone
  Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → NutriSnap.</p>

  <h2>Deleting Your Data</h2>
  <p>Delete the app from your iPhone to permanently remove all stored data. You can also revoke
   HealthKit access in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → NutriSnap.</p>

  <h2>Children</h2>
  <p>NutriSnap is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect
  information from anyone under 18.</p>

  <h2>Medical Disclaimer</h2>
  <p>NutriSnap is for informational and general wellness purposes only. It is not medical
  advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before making
  significant dietary changes.</p>

  <h2>Changes</h2>
  <p>We will notify you of material changes via an in-app notice. Continued use constitutes
  acceptance.</p>
